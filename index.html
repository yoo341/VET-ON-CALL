🚨 Emergency

Go to a vet clinic or call now if your animal has:
  • Trouble breathing, blue/pale gums, or collapse
  • Seizures or sudden weakness
  • Suspected poisoning
  • Heavy bleeding, deep wounds or broken bones
  • Swollen, hard belly with retching (bloat)
  • Straining but unable to pass urine
  • Heatstroke, or labour lasting too long
